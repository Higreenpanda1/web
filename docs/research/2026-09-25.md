## What the market says

The week's dominant signal is freight. The Strait of Hormuz has been effectively closed to routine container shipping since 28 February 2026 (a June reopening collapsed in early July), so China–Gulf cargo lands at Jeddah, King Abdullah Port and Khor Fakkan and moves overland: Xeneta puts China–Jeddah spot rates up 256% since February and China–Khor Fakkan up 479%, Jebel Ali 40ft held at USD 5,860–8,204 in September, and Maersk's published land-bridge haulage to Dammam/Riyadh is USD 1,400 per 20ft and 2,700 per 40ft (22 August 2026). Arabic business media (Al Eqtisadiah, Al Modon 18 September) carry the same story, and air freight is quoted at USD 5.5/kg (+60% month on month). Second: the calendar. Golden Week closes factories 1–7 October, the 140th Canton Fair runs 15 October–4 November in three phases, Singles Day is 11 November, White Friday is 27 November and Chinese New Year 2027 falls on 6 February — importers are deciding now what to pay and ship before each gate. Third: compliance. SABER moved to the 12-digit HS code synchronised with ZATCA in 2026 and extended the Ministry of Industry product-declaration requirement to more categories (VATupdate, 26 August 2026); fees are about SAR 500 per product certificate and SAR 350 per shipment certificate. Fourth: travel. China extended visa-free entry for Saudi, Omani, Kuwaiti and Bahraini passports (30 days, business included) to 31 December 2026, and the UAE and Qatar have reciprocal visa-free entry, which changes the Canton Fair trip for the whole GCC.
