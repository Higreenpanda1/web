## What the market says (October 2026)

- **Hormuz is still nearly closed.** Maersk's update 51 (8 October 2026) still accepts dry cargo for Kuwait, transhipped via Salalah and Khor Fakkan. Emergency freight surcharges have applied since 2 March 2026.
- **Omani ports are absorbing the diverted cargo.** In H1 2026, Sohar handled 545,000 TEU (+40%) and Salalah 2.33 million TEU (+15%).
- **Europe's trade defence keeps tightening on Chinese goods.**
  - Ceramic tableware moved to a single 79% duty from 7 February 2026.
  - Tyres carry duties of 4.3% to 45.3% from 8 July 2026, and valine 31.3% to 53.8%.
  - The EU had 232 trade defence measures in force at the end of 2025 (Commission annual report, 17 September 2026).
- **CBAM is now in its definitive regime.** The Q3 2026 certificate price was €82.32. The first declaration is due 30 September 2027. The UK CBAM starts 1 January 2027.
- **EU product safety alerts hit a record.** Safety Gate received 4,671 alerts in 2025, the highest since 2003. Euronews reports about 43% concerned products from China.
- **Payments.** Foreign cards on Alipay and WeChat Pay are limited to USD 5,000 per transaction and USD 50,000 a year (Shanghai government, 21 September 2026).
- **Saudi Arabia.** From 1 October 2026, products under 10 technical regulations must carry the supplier's name and commercial registration number (SABER).
- **Algeria.** Since July 2025, importers need a provisional import programme, and since May 2026 bank domiciliation must come before shipment (Bank of Algeria note 01/DGC/2026).
- **Visas.** Egypt, Jordan, Morocco and Iraq are on none of China's visa-free lists, so their traders need an M visa. The GCC lists run to 31 December 2026.

## Caveats the writers recorded

- **Ceramic tableware (79%).** The figure comes from the recital of Regulation (EU) 2026/274 and from GTAI; the operative article could not be opened.
- **Kuwait.** The KUCAS start date comes from a Bureau Veritas datasheet. The land-route times come from DHL's update of 6 May 2026 and are dated in the text.
- **Algeria.** The 60% duty band was found only in PwC. The Bank of Algeria note is reported through Algérie Eco and IHK.
- **UAE.** No official source says whether the G-Mark replaces ECAS for toys and low-voltage goods; the article advises planning for both.
- **Private label.** The MOQ ranges come from Alibaba.com's 2026 OEM guide and are labelled as apparel ranges. No tooling costs are given.
- **China share of EU Safety Gate alerts.** Attributed to Euronews, because the official breakdown page could not be reached.
- **Visa article.** Fees and timing for Jordan, Morocco and Iraq are not given; readers are sent to their Chinese mission.
